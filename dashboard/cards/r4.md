# How the R4 calendar bot works

**One line:** buy the known early-month drift windows, hold for the window, exit on the
clock. No signal, no target, no stop — the calendar is the edge.

## Windows (UTC, only when the day of month is 1–14)
- **R4_ETH** — Tuesday 20:00 → Wednesday 20:00 (Wednesday must be day ≤ 14).
- **R4_ETH_V2** — Wednesday and Friday 04:00 → 14:00.
- **R4_BTC** (Monday 06:00 → 18:00) and **R4_BTC_V2** (Wednesday and Friday 04:00 → 14:00)
  are wired but **disabled since 2026-09-12**: only the ETH windows trade.

Weeks 3–5 and bear regimes fire nothing. Wednesdays in weeks 1–2 can hold two positions at
once (the ETH Tuesday trade plus the ETH V2); three only if the BTC V2 window is re-enabled.

## Sizing
Each variant gets 20% of the bot's capital times the sleeve's own leverage stack (2.5× inner
leverage, halved to 1× when 30-day BTC vol is in its top quartile, times a 50%-vol target
capped 1.5–3.0× by regime). A single fire is at most 1.5× capital, usually about 1×; the sum
of open R4 positions is capped at 3× capital and a late fire is scaled down to what is left.

## Character
Expect silence on ~80% of days (at most six ETH windows a month) and nothing at all in the
second half of the month. A fire
more than 5 minutes after the window opens is deliberately skipped and logged as
`missed_window` (cold fills after restarts were the only losers in the old paper run).
Exits land on the first tick after the window closes. Losers are ordinary: the windows
routinely dip 2–3% before the drift completes, which is why no stop-loss survived the
calibration sweep.
