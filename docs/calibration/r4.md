**Bot** (`bots/r4/config.py`): variant `bot_r4_v1`, $10,000 paper, **ETH windows only**
(`ENABLED`: R4_ETH + R4_ETH_V2 on, both BTC windows off since 2026-09-12). Sizing = capital × **0.20 per variant** × the sleeve's stacked leverage capped at
**7.5×** (a fire is ≤ 1.5× capital, typically ~1×); the legacy regime `WEIGHT_TABLE` and
`CORE_ALLOC_CAP` are not used (they bounded the shared legacy variant). **Co-fire gross cap
3.0× capital**: a new fire is scaled down to the remaining budget, never skipped, unless the
remainder is below $250 (`budget_exhausted`). **Late-entry grace 300 s**: a fire more than
5 min after the window opens is logged as `missed_window` (diag JSONL + heartbeat note) and
never taken; this also covers the sleeve's missing after-window guard on R4_ETH. **No
stop-loss** (see deviation below). 60 s ticks. Stale policy: mgmt = btc_1m + eth_1m; entry =
cd_spot_binance + ca_long_short_ratio (the today_inputs stack). Costs at close:
`trades.py` defaults, 10 bp fee + 5 bp slippage + funding.

**Deviation from the per-sleeve SL policy** (`studies/notebooks/r4_bot_prep/findings.md`,
pre-registered 2026-09-06): stops of 2/3/5% were swept on 1m bars over 2020→2026. No level
kept expectancy within 5 bp AND cut the worst fire by ≥ 30% in both eras on every window;
2% stops hit on 10–50% of fires and cut post-ETF expectancy by 4–63 bp. The windows are
10–24 h drift harvests that routinely dip 2–3% before completing (same as PDO). Worst
single fires without a stop: −4.8% (BTC) to −10.2% (ETH pre-ETF) gross, i.e. −7.2% to −15.3%
on the variant at ≤ 1.5× capital, bounded by the scheduled exit and the 3.0× gross cap.
Re-open only via a new pre-registered study.

**Aggregate risk, measured 2026-09-09** (corrects two errors above: capital per fire is 1.5×,
not 1.9×, and up to **three** windows overlap, not two — R4_ETH V1 spans Tue 20:00 → Wed 20:00

## 2026-09-12 — ETH windows only, back in the fleet (paper)

**Decision (user, 2026-09-12):** run R4 in paper with only the ETH windows. The hold of
2026-09-09 (a calendar anomaly with no mechanism for why it works or decays) is lifted for the
ETH pair as *paper evidence gathering*, not as a deployment verdict; the BTC windows stay
disabled.

**Why the ETH pair and not the BTC pair.** `studies/notebooks/r4_study/findings.md`: the
R4_BTC Monday window is post-Binance-perp *emergent* (§1: absent before the perp era, so it
cannot be told apart from a regime artefact), whereas R4_ETH Tue 20:00 → Wed 20:00 is
*era-stable* (§4: positive in both eras and ranked first in both; +1.82 %/fire post-ETF, n = 55,
t = 3.1), and R4_ETH_V2 is the ETH leg of the era-stable Wed+Fri 04→14 cell (+0.56 %/fire,
n = 111, t = 2.8). In the legacy paper run (2026-04-06 → 05-13, orchestrator path) R4_ETH
closed 3 fires for +2.03 % in total and R4_ETH_V2 2 fires for −2.04 %; both V2 losers were the
+127-minute cold fills that the 300 s grace now excludes.

**What changes in the bot.** `ENABLED[R4_BTC] = ENABLED[R4_BTC_V2] = False`; sizing, grace,
gross cap and the no-stop policy are unchanged (weight 0.20 per variant, a fire ≤ 1.5× capital).
With two windows the worst overlap is Wednesday (ETH V1 from Tuesday plus ETH V2), so open
gross is ≤ 2 × 1.5× = 3.0× capital, exactly the cap. Measured 2026-09-12 on 2020→2026 hourly
data at the maximum leg size (1.5× each): the worst same-day ETH cluster is **−24.9 %** of
variant capital (2022-11-08, FTX week: V1 −9.1 %, V2 −7.5 %; the entry day was classified
*uncertain*, so the bear gate did not skip it), then −21.7 % (2020-09-01) and −19.5 %
(2024-01-02); 147 such clusters, 39 of them in bear regimes. The three-leg −21.0 % figure
above was at the sweep's actual stacked sizing and its two ETH legs were the bulk of it, so
dropping the BTC leg does not shrink the tail materially. The worst single ETH fire remains
−10.2 % gross pre-ETF (−15.3 % of variant capital at 1.5×). Note that the vol-target inside
`today_inputs()` is computed on BTC daily returns while ETH's realised vol has run 1.3–1.5×
BTC's (1.5–2× on bull days since the ETFs), so an ETH leg carries proportionally more vol than
the 50 % target implies. `next_windows` (runner log, dashboard panel) lists enabled windows only;
`monitor.HELD_UNITS` is empty again and `start_fleet.ps1` starts r4 by default. The `variants`
row was registered as "Bot: R4 calendar (BTC+ETH)"; `botlib.ensure_bot_variant` now refreshes
`short_name` from the config on start, so the label becomes "Bot: R4 calendar (ETH windows)"
on the first start after this change.

**Regime and asset check (2026-09-12, exploratory scratch computation, not a study; 11
conditioner cells tried, none reaches |t| 1):** on 2020→2026 hourly data the three calendar
cells are a market-wide effect, not an asset effect — on identical windows BTC and ETH move
the same way on 82–85 % of fires, ETH ≈ BTC × 1.2 plus a Tuesday-specific extra (+44 bp/fire,
t 2.1), and the ETH excess over the same hours in weeks 3–5 clears t 2.4–3.4 for the ETH pair.
The in-window mean is positive in every regime state (ETH pooled: strong_bull +83 bp,
uncertain +72, bear +51), so the calendar drift is not regime-conditional; the bear gate
(inherited from the J+ weight table) skips fires with mean +55 bp but a fatter left tail
(p5 −4.8 % vs −3.3 %, share below −3 % 13 % vs 6 %, negative in 2022 / 2024 / 2026) —
**kept**. No pre-specified conditioner (regime, vol gate, prior 1 d / 7 d BTC return,
day-of-month 1–7 vs 8–14, ETH or BTC funding sign, previous fire's sign, ETF era) separates
good fires from bad, so there is no accuracy dial to turn; the levers that matter remain the
cell, the asset and the entry latency.

**Acceptance and re-cut, unchanged in substance:** ≥ 20 fires (≈ 3–4 months at ≤ 6 ETH windows
a month; zero in bear regimes); every `entry_latency_s ≤ 300`; scheduled exits within 180 s;
weekly `strategy_health --variant bot_r4_v1`; disable a window when its 90-day expectancy is
< 0 with n ≥ 8 for three consecutive weekly reviews. Re-enabling a BTC window needs a
mechanism argument, not a better backtest (the 2026-09-09 bar stands).
