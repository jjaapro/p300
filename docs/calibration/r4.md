Inside the sleeve: inner leverage 2.5× (1.0× when the 30d-vol gate fires), times the
vol-target leverage from `jplus_inputs.today_inputs()` (regime caps 1.5–3.0×), regime
sign kept (bear ⇒ no trade). Exit = scheduled window close only, at the current price on
the first tick after the exit time (`botlib.close_due_trades`).

**Bot** (`bots/r4/config.py`): variant `bot_r4_v1`, $10,000 paper, all four variants
enabled. Sizing = capital × **0.20 per variant** × the sleeve's stacked leverage capped at
**7.5×** (a fire is ≤ 1.5× capital, typically ~1×); the legacy regime `WEIGHT_TABLE` and
`CORE_ALLOC_CAP` are not used (they bounded the shared legacy variant). **Co-fire gross cap
3.0× capital**: a new fire is scaled down to the remaining budget, never skipped, unless the
remainder is below $250 (`budget_exhausted`). **Late-entry grace 300 s**: a fire more than
5 min after the window opens is logged as `missed_window` (diag JSONL + heartbeat note) and
never taken; this also covers the sleeve's missing after-window guard on R4_ETH. **No
stop-loss** (see deviation below). 60 s ticks. Stale policy: mgmt = btc_1m + eth_1m; entry =
cd_spot_binance + ca_long_short_ratio (the today_inputs stack). Costs at close:
`trades.py` defaults, 10 bp fee + 5 bp slippage + funding.

**Deviation from the per-sleeve SL policy** (`studies/notebooks/r4_bot_prep/findings.md`,
pre-registered 2026-09-06): stops of 2/3/5% were swept on 1m bars over 2020→2026. No level
kept expectancy within 5 bp AND cut the worst fire by ≥ 30% in both eras on every window;
2% stops hit on 10–50% of fires and cut post-ETF expectancy by 4–63 bp. The windows are
10–24 h drift harvests that routinely dip 2–3% before completing (same as PDO). Worst
single fires without a stop: −4.8% (BTC) to −10.2% (ETH pre-ETF) gross, i.e. −7.2% to −15.3%
on the variant at ≤ 1.5× capital, bounded by the scheduled exit and the 3.0× gross cap.
Re-open only via a new pre-registered study.

**Aggregate risk, measured 2026-09-09** (corrects two errors above: capital per fire is 1.5×,
not 1.9×, and up to **three** windows overlap, not two — R4_ETH V1 spans Tue 20:00 → Wed 20:00
and covers both V2 fires). Worst overlapping cluster in the sweep, as a percent of variant
capital after the 3.0× gross cap: **pre-ETF −21.0%** (2022-11-08, three legs, FTX week) and
**post-ETF −15.2%** (2026-06-05, two legs), against −9.5% for the same clusters under a 3%
stop. The stop sweep's pre-registered rule tested the worst *single* fire and is silent on
clusters, so `STOP_LOSS_PCT = None` stands as calibrated — but the operator should decide
knowingly, since the era the bot trades carries a measured ~15% single-day variant drawdown.

**Late-entry provenance**: post-ETF loss versus entering at the open is ≤ 5 bp at +5 min on
all four windows and > 5 bp at +10 min on R4_ETH (11 bp); the rule's floor is 5 min. The
2026-05-13 cold fills (+127 min) were the only V2 paper losers.

**Monitoring / disable rule**: weekly
`python -m strategies.support.strategy_health --variant bot_r4_v1 --capital 10000`.
Disable a variant (`ENABLED[...] = False` + a row below) when its 90-day expectancy is
< 0 with n ≥ 8 for three consecutive weekly reviews. Baselines: the post-ETF table above.

**Paper acceptance** (execution fidelity, ~7 weeks): ≥ 20 fires; every
`entry_latency_s ≤ 300` (in `trades.notes`); scheduled exits within 180 s of `exit_time`;
zero entries outside the grace; zero duplicate (strategy, day) rows; monitor green;
`missed_window` count with causes from `bots/r4/logs/diag.jsonl`.

**Known sleeve gap (not fixed here)**: `r4_eth_decide` has no after-window check
(`signal.py:217`); the bot's guard covers it, the legacy orchestrator path does not.
Sleeve fix needs a go-ahead.
