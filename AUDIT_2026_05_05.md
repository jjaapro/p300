# P-300 Aggressive 2.0 — Follow-up Audit (2026-05-05)

Open items surfaced while validating `AUDIT_2026_05_04.md`. Each was claimed `[x]` in the prior audit but verification found the fix incomplete.

## High Severity

- [x] **Lookahead canary still missing for 5 of 6 tactical sleeves** — `tests/test_jplus_lookahead.py` adds coverage for ADX (line 97) and `regime_classifier` (line 129), but Carry, Thu Bear, PDO, CPR, and FOMC remain uncovered. Each has its own data loaders and signal computation; without a dual-clock test, a future change can silently introduce lookahead in any of them. (`tests/test_jplus_lookahead.py`)

## Medium Severity

- [ ] **`variant_registry._record_event` close not in `finally`** — Audit claimed this was fixed alongside the other connection-leak sites, but `con.close()` at `services/variant_registry.py:175` sits inside the `try` block, not a `finally`. An exception in `con.execute` bypasses close and leaks the connection. The other 5 named sites (`variant_engine._shadow_trade_exists`, `_create_shadow_trade`, `trade_db.set_config`, `risk_caps._open_btc_long_alloc_pct`, plus `persist_close` on the close path) are correctly wrapped. (`services/variant_registry.py:164-178`)

- [ ] **FOMC `try_fire_for_variant` has no `None` guard on observer row** — DB path was fixed to `db.TRADER_DB` (line 451), but the read at lines 459-463 still does `r["decision"]` with no `if r is None` check. A `_has_observer_record` precheck protects it under normal flow, so the crash is reachable only on a concurrent delete between check and read — but the audit explicitly called out this guard and it was not added. (`services/fomc_service.py:459-463`)

The positive claim is missing — operators reading the docstring can't confirm cash allocation from it. (`run.py:3-13`)
